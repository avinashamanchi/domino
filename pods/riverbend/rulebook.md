# Riverbend General · Kidney Transplant Program · SW-TX-07 (synthetic)

### §1.1 Infection
A recipient must be 7 days afebrile and off IV antibiotics before transplant.

### §1.2 Travel
Donors travelling more than 100 miles need a scheduled travel day before surgery.

### §2.1 Arrival cutoff
Organs from outside programs must arrive by 13:00.

### §2.2 Cold ischemia
Maximum cold ischemia is 12 hours.

### §3.1 No local match
When no reciprocal match exists inside Riverbend, the program may forward a de-identified request to partner regions under regional sharing agreement RSA-3, looking for a third pair that can close a loop. Partner regions under RSA-3: Sierra Nevada (Reno); Central Valley (Fresno); Pacific Northwest (Portland).

### §3.2 What may leave Riverbend
Names, charts, antibody lists and donor notes never leave Riverbend. Outside programs receive a request id, a candidate token and fixed categories only.
