# Harbor Point Hospital · Living Donor Program · HPH-LD-12 (synthetic)

### §1.1 Donor work-up
The donor's evaluation must be complete and less than 12 months old at the time of surgery.

### §1.2 Recipient status
The recipient must be active on the waiting list with no open infection.

### §2.1 Paired exchange across hospitals
Harbor Point may join a paired exchange with an outside program when blood types are compatible and the virtual crossmatch is negative in both directions. Both receiving surgeons must sign an approval before scheduling.

### §2.2 Cold ischemia
Maximum cold ischemia is 10 hours, or 6 hours if the recipient's cPRA is 80% or higher.

### §2.3 Implant timing
Implant must begin by 14:00.

### §3.1 What may leave Harbor Point
Patient names, charts, antibody lists and donor notes never leave Harbor Point. Replies to outside programs use a request id, an opaque candidate token and fixed categories only.
