# Alder Medical Center · Kidney Transplant Program · AMC-KTP-04 (synthetic)

### §1.1 Donor work-up
A living donor's evaluation must be complete and less than 12 months old on the day of surgery.

### §2.1 Paired exchange
Alder may propose a paired exchange with an outside program. Requests sent outside Alder carry tokens, blood types, donor HLA typing and the recipient's unacceptable antigens only. Names and charts never leave Alder.

### §2.4 Surgeon review
Every exchange proposal must be reviewed by the recipient's transplant surgeon and approved on a signed approval form before anything is scheduled.

### §3.2 Cold ischemia
Maximum cold ischemia is 12 hours, or 8 hours if the recipient's cPRA is 80% or higher.

### §3.3 Operating rooms
Donor surgeries start no earlier than 07:30.
