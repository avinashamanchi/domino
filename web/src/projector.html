The question

What if the person who could help your patient is at another hospital?

But hospitals can't look at each other's patients. Their records are private, and they should stay that way.

The network

Three hospitals. Three private pods.

Each hospital keeps its patients in its own data pod, on its own machine. A Flower network connects their agents. Records never move; questions and short answers do.
